Carbon Emission & Sustainability Score Formulas

Official mathematical methodology used in EcoTrack calculations

Formula 1

Carbon Emission Calculation Formula (kg CO₂e)

Carbon Footprint (kg CO₂e) = Input Quantity × Emission Factor

Calculates the exact greenhouse gas footprint for logged activities based on IPCC / DEFRA standards:

Transportation (Petrol Car): Miles × 0.35 kg CO₂e
Electricity (Grid Power): kWh × 0.39 kg CO₂e
Cooking Fuel (LPG Gas): kg × 2.05 kg CO₂e
Tree Plantation (Offset): Trees × -22.00 kg CO₂e (Deduction)
Formula 2

Overall Sustainability Score Formula (Out of 100)

Overall Sustainability Score = Math.round( Sum(11 Category Scores + Goal Score) / 12 )

Rates user eco-performance on a 0 – 100 scale using a 3-part sub-engine:

A
Emission Categories Score (0–100):

Score = Math.max(30, 85 - (Logged Emissions - Daily Baseline) * 0.8)

Lower emissions increase score towards 100.
B
Green Offsets Bonus (0–100):

Score = Math.min(100, 85 + Total Offsets (kg CO2e) * 0.5)

Tree planting, solar energy, and recycling add bonus points.
C
Goal Progress Score (0–100):

Score = Math.min(100, 50 + (Completed Goals / Total Goals) * 40 + Avg Progress % * 0.6)

Completing sustainability goals directly increases Goal Score and overall score.
